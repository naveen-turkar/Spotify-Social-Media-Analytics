# 🎧 Social Media Analytics for Strategic Branding — Spotify

Excel-based social media intelligence solution built for Spotify, analyzing engagement, platform performance, hashtag strategy, follower growth, and campaign effectiveness to drive data-backed marketing decisions.

## 📌 Project Overview

Spotify runs multiple music-driven campaigns and artist collaborations across Instagram, Twitter, Facebook, and YouTube to grow its listener base and strengthen its brand. As a Data Analyst, this project consolidates Spotify's social media data to answer:

- Which content types and artists drive the most engagement?
- Where should advertising budget be allocated effectively?
- What influences follower growth and app downloads?
- How do social campaigns correlate with premium subscriptions?

## 🎯 Objective

Build a comprehensive **Social Media Intelligence Solution in Excel** that consolidates Spotify's social data and delivers actionable insights to enhance content strategy, optimize platform investments, and evaluate campaign success.

## 🗂️ Dataset

The analysis uses three datasets from the Spotify Marketing Dataset:

| Dataset | Description |
|---|---|
| **Posts** | Social media post-level details — platform, post type, artist tag, likes, comments, shares, impressions, hashtags |
| **Engagement Summary** | Weekly aggregated metrics per platform — ad spend, follower growth, clicks, impressions |
| **Campaign Metadata** | Details of major campaigns (e.g., ChillVibes, Wrapped 2024) including start/end dates and objectives |

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel** — Data cleaning, Pivot Tables, formulas, charts, correlation analysis

## 🔍 Approach

**1. Data Preprocessing & Cleaning**
- Removed duplicate post entries
- Standardized date and platform formats
- Corrected numeric formatting for likes, impressions, ad spend, downloads
- Split multi-hashtag columns into separate entries

**2. Engagement Analysis**
- Calculated Engagement Rate per platform: `(Likes + Comments + Shares) / Impressions`
- Identified top 10 posts by engagement rate
- Built Pivot Tables for likes/shares/comments by content type & platform, and average clicks by hashtag
- Ranked top-performing hashtags using ranking formulas

**3. Platform Performance Analysis**
- Compared engagement and weekly follower growth across platforms
- Visualized Ad Spend vs. Engagement using charts
- Recommended platform focus based on data

**4. Hashtag & Content Strategy**
- Identified most frequent hashtags and their average engagement
- Compared content type (Text, Story, Reel) performance across platforms
- Recommended optimal content type–platform combinations

**5. Campaign Effectiveness**
- Computed total & average impressions, likes, clicks per campaign
- Measured engagement uplift during vs. before each campaign
- Evaluated campaign ROI (engagement/ad spend) and follower/download growth impact

**6. Follower Retention & Loyalty**
- Identified the week with the highest net follower gain
- Built a moving average chart to analyze follower trends
- Analyzed correlation between Ad Spend and Follower Growth/App Downloads
